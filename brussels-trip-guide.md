## Activities & Things to Do

### Must-See Landmarks

#### Grand Place ⭐
One of the most beautiful medieval squares in Europe and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Features ornate 17th-century Gothic and Baroque buildings with spectacular guildhouses, City Hall, and Maison du Roi. **Beautifully lit after dark** - perfect for an evening stroll!

#### Les Galeries Royales Saint-Hubert
Gorgeous glass-roofed arcade in the city center, lined with cafes, theaters, and luxury stores. Great for browsing and people-watching.

#### The Atomium
Brussels' futuristic landmark featuring stainless-steel spheres and connecting tubes with exhibits and viewpoints. A unique architectural experience.

### Cultural Experiences

#### Comic Strip Murals 🎨
Brussels is the capital of the comic strip! Since 1991, the city has been gradually covered with over 80 murals depicting famous Belgian comic characters. Follow the comic strip route through the city.

#### Cantillon Brewery
Founded in 1900, this is the only operational lambic brewery in Brussels. A must-visit for beer enthusiasts to see traditional brewing methods.

#### Choco-Story Brussels 🍫
Interactive chocolate museum with live demonstrations, tastings, and bean-to-bar processes. Belgium is famous for its chocolate - don't miss this!

### Hidden Gems & Off-Beaten-Path

#### St. Gilles & Matongé Neighborhoods
- **St. Gilles**: One of the coolest parts of the city with traditional Belgian bars and outdoor seating
- **Parvis de St. Gilles**: Quirky city square with plenty of character
- **Matongé**: Completely different atmosphere, drenched in African culture with colorful shops and bars

#### Rue de la Cigogne
A side street "that belongs on Pinterest" - escapes modernization and gentrification. Best time to visit: April-June when wisteria takes over the street.

#### Art Nouveau Architecture
Brussels has 500+ Art Nouveau buildings, many in Ixelles and Uccle neighborhoods. Perfect for architecture lovers!

#### The Black Tower
Just 10 minutes from Grand Place, built in the 13th century to defend the city.

#### In 't Spinnekopke (The Spider's Web)
Brussels' oldest restaurant, founded in 1762. Still somewhat of a secret!

### Evening Activities & Nightlife

#### Beer Bars
- **Delirium Café**: Over 2,000 types of beer from around the world, in a quaint alleyway with lively atmosphere
- **L'Archiduc**: Historic 1930s Art Deco bar with intimate atmosphere and live jazz performances

#### Nightlife Districts
- **Saint-Géry District**: Trendy hotspot with lively squares, numerous cafes, bars, and younger bohemian crowd
- **Ixelles (Place du Châtelain & Flagey)**: More sophisticated nightlife with stylish wine bars and gourmet cocktail spots

#### Clubs (if you're in the mood)
- **Fuse Club**: Premier techno club, one of Europe's best for electronic music
- **Spirito**: Nightclub in a converted church with opulent decor
- **C-12**: Underground venue beneath Brussels Central station combining club nights with art installations

#### Théâtre Royal de Toone
Historic puppet theater with traditional shows in various languages, plus a cozy bar serving local beers.

## Restaurant Recommendations

### Fine Dining

#### Woodpeck 47
Near the Grand Place, one of the best gourmet addresses in downtown Brussels.

#### Sir Kwinten
Fine dining with stylish decor, welcoming atmosphere, and top-notch food.

#### Les Frères Romano
Highly rated (9.4/10), excellent choice for a special dinner.

### Traditional Belgian Cuisine 🇧🇪

#### Au Vieux Saint Martin
Serving Belgian cuisine since 1968. Popular destination for authentic Belgian taste.

#### La Buvette
Cozy restaurant in a former butcher shop where Chef Nicolas Scheidt serves creative Belgian dishes using traditional products.

#### Le Plattesteen
Best Belgian cuisine with typical Bruxellois interiors, generous portions, simple food, and local produce.

#### Leon (for Moules-Frites)
Famous restaurant popular with tourists and locals for reasonably-priced mussels and fries - the classic Belgian pairing!

### Other Highly Rated Options

- **Cave du Roy**: Popular choice
- **Sushi Wa**: For when you need a break from Belgian food

### Must-Try Belgian Specialties
- **Moules-Frites**: Mussels and fries - the iconic Belgian dish
- **Belgian Waffles**: Try them fresh from a local spot
- **Belgian Chocolate**: World-renowned quality
- **Belgian Beer**: Hundreds of varieties to explore
- **Belgian Fries**: Often served with various sauces

## Practical Tips

### Getting Around
Brussels has good public transport (metro, trams, buses). The city center is walkable, and many attractions are close together.

### Best Neighborhoods to Explore
1. **City Center**: Grand Place, Galeries Saint-Hubert
2. **Ixelles**: Art Nouveau architecture, sophisticated dining and nightlife
3. **St. Gilles**: Traditional bars, bohemian atmosphere
4. **Matongé**: African culture and vibrancy

### Note
⚠️ The Royal Palace is closed for renovations through 2024; visits resume summer 2026.
